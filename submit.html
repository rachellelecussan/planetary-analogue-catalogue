What happens next

  1. Your submission goes to the catalogue's maintainer.
  2. A reviewer checks the science, sources and safety rating.
  3. They approve it or contact you about changes.
  4. Approved sites appear on the map and in the next citable release, with you credited.

Good entries

  • Back every number with a source.
  • Say where the analogue breaks down, not just where it works.
  • Rate hazards against the written scales, not gut feel.
